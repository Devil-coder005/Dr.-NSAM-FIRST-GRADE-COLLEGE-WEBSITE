Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Nitte was founded in 1979 by committed philanthropist and legal luminary, Justice K.S. Hegde, who strongly believed that education was pivotal to the progress of a community. President of the Trust, Mr. N.V. Hegde, and Advisor Dr. N.R. Shetty — an eminent educationist — both share the same noble vision.

Dr. NSAM First Grade College (Bengaluru) is a NAAC accredited institution under Nitte (Deemed to be University), Mangalore. In January 2019, the college relocated to a new campus with aesthetically designed academic blocks set within a sprawling three-acre green environment. Students consistently excel in academics, sports, and extracurricular activities.

🔭 Vision

To establish excellence in academic standards and to make learners employable and responsible citizens contributing to the progress of the nation and the world.

🎯 Mission

To develop the institution as a centre of excellence, imparting quality education that generates competent and skilled human resources with social conscience and high ethical standards.
